# Ganapa Day 3

Today was a great day for me.

I completed my project synopsis, submitted it to my college as a student, and it was approved. That approval gave me a different kind of confidence because something I had been planning and working on has now moved one step forward.

From today, I am also starting work on a new website project called Namma Tumakuru. I am building it with my team, and we are working together to turn the idea into something useful and beautiful. This is another project where I am learning by actually building instead of only thinking about the idea.

Today I also spent time thinking deeply about Namma Icons and why we are building it.

As the founder of Namma Icons, I don't want it to be just another website selling posters. I want to understand why Kannada people and people from Karnataka would connect with what we are creating.

The idea behind Namma Icons is connected to our Kannada identity, our culture, our people, our memories, our cinema, our icons, and the things that make us feel connected to Karnataka.

I have been working on the project, the wall posters, the content, and the ideas behind the brand. I am also thinking about how we can explain the reason behind Namma Icons in a way that feels real to Kannada people instead of simply trying to sell something.

I want the content to make people stop, think, remember something, and feel connected.

Today made me realise something about being a founder.

Building a project is not only about creating the website or the product. It is also about understanding why the project exists, who it is for, what problem or feeling it is trying to solve, and how the team can turn that idea into something real.

I am happy that my team is working with me on these new projects. There is still a lot to learn and a lot to build, but that is what makes this journey exciting.

I will also continue creating quotes and content around these ideas and documenting what I learn along the way.

The quotes I create today will be added to this update as part of the journey.

For now, my main thought is:

**A project becomes meaningful when you know why you are building it.**

I am building, learning, leading, making mistakes, solving problems, and continuing forward.

This is only another step in the journey.
