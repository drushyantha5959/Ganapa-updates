# Ganapa Day 1

Today I decided to start something that I have wanted to do for a long time.

I want to keep a record of the real work I do every day.

Not just the good results. I want to write about the problems too. I want to remember what I tried, what failed, what I fixed, what I learned, and what I want to do better the next day.

A big part of my work today was Namma Icons.

I have been working on the SEO of the website, and I have been finding and fixing different SEO problems. I am also working on blogs because I want people to actually discover the brand through search. The more I work on it, the more I understand that SEO is not something I can finish in one sitting. It is something I have to keep learning and improving.

At the same time, college is going on. I am in my final year of BCA, and I am working on my college project, Smart City Tumkur. I am trying to connect what I learn in college with something practical instead of keeping everything only inside my notes.

I am also spending time on IGD, Influencers Growth Doctor. There I work on things like editing, portfolios, social media, digital marketing, and creative ideas.

Sometimes I look at everything I am doing and realise that there are many things happening at the same time. I am studying, building projects, working on brands, learning new skills, editing, thinking about ideas, and trying to solve problems whenever they come up.

It can be difficult to manage everything, but honestly, these real situations are teaching me more than simply reading about something.

One achievement that I am especially proud of is becoming a Meta Early Access member. I have also worked on content and collaborations that have reached millions of views.

Those numbers are exciting, but I don't want this repository to be only about numbers.

I want it to show the journey behind them.

I want to look back at these entries one day and see how I was thinking, what I was learning, what mistakes I made, and how I improved.

That is the real reason I am starting Ganapa Updates.

I don't know exactly where this journey will take me. But I know that if I keep learning, building, solving problems, and documenting the process, I will be able to see my own progress clearly.

For today, one thought stays with me.

Real work gives us real problems. Solving those problems is where real learning begins.

Tomorrow, I will continue from here.
