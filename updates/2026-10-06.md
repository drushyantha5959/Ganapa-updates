# Ganapa Day 1

Today is the first day of my Ganapa Updates journey.

I wanted to start documenting the things I actually work on every day. Not just achievements, but the problems I face, the things I learn, the solutions I find, and the ideas that come to my mind while working.

Today, a major part of my work has been around Namma Icons.

I have been working on the SEO of the Namma Icons website. I came across several SEO related problems, and I have been fixing them one by one. Along with that, I am working on blogs and trying to understand how I can make the website easier to discover through search.

I am learning that SEO is not something that can be finished once and forgotten. It needs continuous observation, testing, writing, improving, and learning.

At the same time, I am working on my BCA final year college project, Smart City Tumkur. This is giving me another opportunity to take what I learn in college and apply it to a real project.

I am also working on Influencers Growth Doctor, or IGD. My work there involves portfolios, editing, social media handling, digital marketing, creative ideas, and working with creators and brands.

Sometimes it feels like I am handling many things at the same time. I am a BCA student, I am building projects, I am working with brands, I am learning digital marketing, I am doing editing, and I am constantly thinking about new ideas.

But I also feel that this is where most of my learning is happening.

College gives me the foundation. Real projects give me the problems. Solving those problems gives me experience.

One achievement that means a lot to me is being a Meta Early Access member. I have also worked on content and collaborations that have reached millions of views. I am proud of those achievements, but I want to focus more on what I learned while reaching them.

I want this repository to become a genuine record of that journey.

Every day, I want to write about what I worked on, what went wrong, what I solved, what I learned, and what I want to improve next.

I do not want this to look like a perfect success story. I want it to show the real process of learning and building.

The biggest thought I am taking from today is simple.

Real work creates real problems, and real problems create real learning.

Tomorrow, I will continue from where I stopped today and see what I can learn next.
